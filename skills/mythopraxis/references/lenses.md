# Philosophical and narrative lenses

These are contextual lenses, not universal archetypes or claims that traditions are interchangeable.

| Lens | Useful question | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Stoic assent and control | What can be chosen here, and what cannot? | Do not reduce Stoicism to emotional suppression |
| Aristotelian balance | What pair of qualities prevents opposite excesses? | Do not treat the mean as simple moderation |
| Socratic inquiry | Which assumption has not survived questioning? | Inquiry should clarify, not perform cleverness |
| Daoist non-forcing | Where is effort creating the resistance it fights? | Preserve historical and cultural context |
| Confucian relational responsibility | Which relationships and duties shape the right response? | Do not flatten role ethics into obedience |
| Aesopic fable | Can one consequence make the choice memorable? | Use public-domain sources and original wording |
| Panchatantra narrative reasoning | What practical judgment emerges from nested consequences? | Treat it as a specific literary tradition, not generic folklore |
| Craft and stewardship | What would careful custody require? | Keep metaphors functional rather than romanticized |

Use original scenes inspired by general questions. Cite translations and scholarship in provenance records instead of copying passages.
