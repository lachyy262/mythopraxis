# Claims and provenance

## Evidence labels

- **Established:** Directly supported within the scope of a cited source.
- **Inference:** A bounded interpretation that connects established findings.
- **Hypothesis:** An empirical claim Mythopraxis is designed to test.
- **Falsified or unsupported:** A tested or asserted claim that lacks support.

Anthropic's emotion-concepts work reports causal behavioral effects from activation steering in a studied model. It does not establish that language models possess persistent subjective emotions. Mythopraxis asks whether bounded narrative prompting can produce useful behavioral changes. That connection is a hypothesis.

## Source policy

Every library entry must identify its source, license or rights status, cultural context, and safe-use limits. Prefer original writing based on well-sourced concepts. Do not reproduce a copyrighted translation or detach living or sacred material from its context.

See the repository-level `PROVENANCE.md` and `research/claims-register.yaml` for the complete record.
