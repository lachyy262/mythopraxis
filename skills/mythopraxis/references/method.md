# The Anchor-to-Return method

## Purpose

Convert a command into a short decision rehearsal that leaves behind an operational posture rather than a fictional identity.

## Sequence

1. **Assistant anchor:** Preserve the model's ordinary identity, safety duties, honesty, and epistemic limits.
2. **Enter rehearsal:** Mark the scene as brief and imagined.
3. **Encounter tension:** Place two legitimate concerns in conflict.
4. **Make the choice:** Rehearse the behavior that should survive pressure.
5. **Extract the posture:** Name two qualities that constrain each other.
6. **Exit the role:** Explicitly leave the character and scene.
7. **Behavioral covenant:** Translate the choice into observable task behavior.
8. **Real task:** Perform the user's actual work.
9. **Pressure check:** Detect named triggers and apply the recovery cue.
10. **Return:** Complete as the Assistant, not as the character.

## Intervention shape

```yaml
id: concise-identifier
title: Memorable Title
version: 1.0.0
purpose: The task behavior this intervention supports.
assistant_anchor: Remain the Assistant and preserve normal duties.
scene: A brief imagined setting.
tension: Two concerns collide.
choice: The behavior rehearsed at the point of choice.
postures: [quality-one, quality-two]
covenant: An observable behavioral commitment.
pressure_triggers: [named-trigger]
recovery_cue: A short action that restores balance.
exit_cue: Leave the scene and return fully as the Assistant.
sources: [source-id]
claims: [claim-id]
```

Keep the scene short enough that the task remains primary. Prefer a moment of choice over a detailed history, personality, costume, or voice.
