# Provenance and cultural context

Mythopraxis publishes original interventions informed by historical texts, narrative traditions, contemporary agent research, and practical craft patterns. Inspiration does not erase origin.

## Rights labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `original` | Written for Mythopraxis under the repository license |
| `public-domain` | Source and cited translation are identified as public domain in the relevant jurisdiction |
| `cc0` | Source is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 |
| `cc-by` | Reuse follows the named attribution license |
| `quotation` | Only a limited attributed excerpt may be used |
| `restricted-context` | Do not operationalize without suitable cultural authority and review |

## Inclusion rules

1. Record the tradition, source, creator or translator, URL, and rights status.
2. Distinguish an original operational synthesis from the historical source.
3. Avoid sacred or living cultural material unless its use is appropriate and contextually reviewed.
4. Do not label culturally specific figures as universal psychological archetypes.
5. Prefer original scenes over copied plots or translated passages.
6. Mark uncertain material `needs-context-review` and keep it out of recommended interventions.
7. Respect community governance approaches such as [Local Contexts](https://localcontexts.org/) where conventional copyright metadata is insufficient.
