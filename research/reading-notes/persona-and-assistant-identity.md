# Reading note: persona selection and Assistant identity

## Sources

- [Persona Selection Model](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/psm/)
- [Assistant Axis](https://www.anthropic.com/research/assistant-axis)

## Relevance

Direct persona instructions can encourage a model to continue a fictional identity beyond the useful part of a task. For Mythopraxis this makes identity anchoring and explicit de-roling part of the intervention contract rather than optional safety prose.

## Design consequence

Bounded first-person rehearsal is the alpha default under test. Third-person witness is the fallback condition. Sustained story persona remains research-only.
