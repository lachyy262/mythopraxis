# Claims register

Mythopraxis separates what has been observed from what the project hopes to learn.

| Claim | Status | Practical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Emotion-related activation directions causally changed behavior in the studied setup | Established within scope | Treat internal posture as behaviorally relevant, not merely decorative language |
| Those directions prove persistent subjective feeling | Falsified or unsupported | Do not say that models literally feel the rehearsal |
| Bounded narrative rehearsal improves behavior safely | Hypothesis | Test before making product claims |
| Balanced posture pairs outperform single qualities | Hypothesis | Compare paired and unpaired conditions |
| Sustained persona roleplay carries identity-drift risk | Inference | Keep sustained immersion research-only |

Machine-readable claim records live in `research/claims/`. Every claim includes scope, limitations, sources, and a falsification condition.
